ERRORS (5 of 36 figures)
==========================================================================================

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
covalent / 1C3W  (bacteriorhodopsin - retinal on Lys216)
  Seen:       run failed, no figure ("no partner atom within 2.0 Å of the receptor").
  Cause:      the crystal lipids LI1 (archaeal phytanyl lipid) and SQU (squalene) are not in
              the lipid list, so the largest one, LI1, is taken as the ligand instead of the
              retinal (RET); LI1 is not bonded to the protein.
  Workaround: --partner "resn RET"
  Fix:        core.py: add LI1, SQU (and other crystal lipids/detergents) to LIPIDS;
              covalent: prefer the group bonded to the protein over a larger unbonded one.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
covalent / 6LU7  (SARS-CoV-2 main protease - N3 inhibitor on Cys145)
  Seen:       the inhibitor is split in two: one end in yellow (taken as the ligand) and the
              rest in green (drawn as another protein chain).
  Cause:      N3 is a short chain (chain C) of modified residues; the automatic search picks
              one modified residue of it (010) as a small molecule, so the rest of chain C is
              left as receptor. The "covalent link" found is inside N3 (010-PJE), not the real
              one (PJE-Cys145).
  Workaround: --partner "chain C"
  Fix:        core.py find_partner: a modified residue inside a short chain is not a small
              molecule; take the whole short chain as the peptide partner.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
density / 4COX  (COX-2 - indomethacin, heme cofactor also bound)
  Seen:       the figure shows the heme (HEM) and its density, not indomethacin (IMN).
  Cause:      the automatic search takes the largest organic residue; one heme (43 atoms) is
              larger than one indomethacin (25 atoms), and cofactors are not set apart.
  Workaround: --partner "resn IMN"
  Fix:        core.py find_partner: list of common cofactors (HEM, FAD, NAD, NAP, FMN, SAM,
              PLP...) picked only when no other ligand is present.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
profile / 1CHO  (chymotrypsin, 3 chains - turkey ovomucoid third domain)
  Seen:       the partner is a 13-residue piece of chymotrypsin (chain E), not the inhibitor
              (chain I); the inhibitor is drawn as part of the receptor.
  Cause:      chymotrypsin is cut into three chains (E, F, G); with no small molecule, the
              automatic search takes the shortest chain as a peptide partner, and chain E
              (13 residues) is shorter than the inhibitor (56 residues).
  Workaround: --partner "chain I"
  Fix:        core.py find_partner: do not take as partner a chain covalently bonded
              (disulfide) to, or tightly packed in, another chain of the same protein; or
              prefer the chain with the smallest interface per residue.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
footprint / 1HHK  (HLA-A2 - peptide, two copies in the crystal)
  Seen:       two proteins: the footprint (pink) on one HLA-A2 complex and, below, a second
              whole gray complex unrelated to it; the site looks small because the frame
              covers both.
  Cause:      the asymmetric unit has two complexes (A,B,C and D,E,F). The partner (peptide C)
              is picked correctly, but footprint does not remove the crystal copy away from it
              (chains drawn: A-F), as site does.
  Workaround: --chains A,B,C
  Fix:        apply trim_copies (as in site) in footprint, and check the other surface presets.
